---
title: "Data sources"
description: "Official sources used by each query namespace."
---

| Source | Interface | Data |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TWSE / TPEx | ISIN directories, official JSON/OpenAPI and TWSE MIS | Securities, history, indices, corporate actions, trading restrictions and quotes |
| Emerging stocks | ISIN, TPEx daily records and recommending-broker XML | Directory, daily history, quotes and restrictions |
| TAIFEX | OpenAPI, historical CSV, product pages and MIS | Futures/options history, contracts, specifications, margins and quotes |
| MOPS | POST `/mops/api/t164sb03`, `t164sb04`, `t164sb05` | Balance sheets, income statements and cash-flow statements |
| MOPS | `t05st10_ifrs`, `t05st09_2` | Monthly revenue and dividends |
| TDCC | `https://opendata.tdcc.com.tw/getOD.ashx?id=1-5` | Shareholding brackets in CSV |
| NDC | Official PMI/NMI CSV and business-cycle ZIP | Monthly observations, business-cycle signals and indicators |
| CBC | `https://cpx.cbc.gov.tw/API/DataAPI/Get?FileName=BP01D01` | Exchange-rate JSON |

MOPS adapters convert Gregorian years to ROC years and preserve account headings
and reporting periods. TDCC records retain publication dates, NDC records retain
observation months, and CBC records retain published currency-pair direction.

Request parameters, source URLs and response hashes are recorded in
`research/source-probes.json`, `research/implementation-live-checks.json` and
`tests/fixtures/source-manifest.json`.
